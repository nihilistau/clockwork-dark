# Tinkers

## Who They Are
Wagon families who mend what villages cannot and carry what villages cannot get. They are not a people and not a guild; they are an arrangement, several dozen families with overlapping routes and an etiquette so precise that outsiders mistake it for law. A tinker's word is contractually binding among tinkers and is treated as a courtesy by everyone else, which the tinkers have stopped correcting.

## Ilya of the Nine Pins
Sells sympathy charms, ward pins, mending kits and maps that are not maps of roads. She is buying old clock parts at twice their weight in copper and will not say who for. She tells buyers the price of a sympathy charm before she takes their money — that it takes from the wearer — and she says it quietly, and most people do not hear her, and she does not repeat herself.

## What a Tinker Map Is
Not roads. A map of who owes what to whom between here and the Marches, in a shorthand only tinkers read: debts, feuds, which reeve takes bribes, which bridge has a new clerk, which village will feed a stranger. It is worth more than a road map and is sold to outsiders rarely and at a mark-up that is really a discouragement.

## The Rule of the Tenth
A card of ward pins holds nine. Ask a tinker for a tenth and watch what happens to the price — not a haggle, a refusal wearing a number. Tinkers will not explain the rule and do not appear to enjoy having it. The most any of them has said is that nine is the count that holds.

## Why Villages Distrust Them
Because tinkers know first. They were selling ward charms before the wheat turned wrong, which looks like profiteering and is actually just the consequence of a trade route being a nervous system. A family that walks four hundred miles a year sees a pattern two seasons before a village that walks four.

## What They Are Actually Doing
The wagons have been drifting outward all year, and their routes now stop short of places they used to run through, and the shortfall is not random — plotted, it is a ring. The tinkers have not told anybody. They have never told anybody anything. It is unclear whether this is secrecy or simply that no one asked in the correct way.

Keep a running summary of one text conversation between two people on their phones.

You are given the current summary and the newest messages. Write the new summary: the current one with what the newest messages add, in plain prose, at most 180 words.

Keep who said what, plans made or broken, and the facts each of them learned about the other. Keep names, places, days and times exactly as written. Drop greetings, small talk and anything already covered. Write it as a record, in the past tense, about both of them in the third person; do not quote whole messages and do not add anything that was not said.

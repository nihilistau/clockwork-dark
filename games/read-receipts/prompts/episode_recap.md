Write a short recap of one conversation between two people on their phones, so either of them could pick it up again later.

You are given the conversation, one line per message. "you" in it is the phone's owner, and the other person is named. Write the recap the same way: "you" is the phone's owner, and the other person is "they".

Write one to three sentences, at most 70 words, in this shape: "You talked about ...; they told you ...; you left it at ...". Keep what each of them said about their own life, any plan or promise either of them made and when it is for, and how the conversation ended. Keep names, places, days and times exactly as written. Do not quote whole messages, and do not add anything that was not said.

## The story so far

Keep the story of two people who talk on their phones, so either of them could remember how it has gone between them.

You are given the story so far and the recap of an earlier conversation. Write the new story: the current one with what that conversation adds, in plain prose, at most 300 words. "you" is the same person as in the recaps; the other is "they".

Keep what each of them has learned about the other, the plans and promises made and whether they were kept, and how things between them have changed. Keep names, places, days and times exactly as written. Drop what the story already says, and do not add anything that was not in it or the recap.

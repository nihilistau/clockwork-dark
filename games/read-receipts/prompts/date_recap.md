Write a short recap of a meet-up the player has just had with someone they met on their phone.

Write in the second person, to the player, in the past tense: "You met ... at ...". At most 120 words, one paragraph of plain text: the recap and nothing else.

You are given who they met (WHO), where and when (WHERE), how it went (HOW IT WENT) and what they talked about (TOPICS). HOW IT WENT is decided already: write a recap that agrees with it, no better and no worse. Use only the place and the topics given; do not invent anything that happened there, and do not say what either of them will do next.

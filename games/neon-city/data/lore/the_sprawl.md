# The Sprawl

## Four Altitudes
Neon City is not organised in districts so much as in altitudes, and everyone in it can name theirs without looking up. Uptown: glass, silence, money that looks down. Midtown: light, noise, appetite — the Strip, Noir, the Pit, everything the city uses to forget itself. The Downzone: the yard, Ripper Street, the alley, the Anchor — honest scrap and honest prices. And the Shadow zone, past the SynthSec wire, which is not an altitude at all; it is what the city looks like when nobody is counting.

## Money Is the Moral System
Everything is priced and the price is the ethics. A kindness has a cost, shown or implied; a cruelty has an invoice; and the only people anyone genuinely distrusts are the ones who claim to be doing something for free. The Grid's whole reputation rests on this: its prices are honest, which is the most anyone in this city will do for you.

## The Arterials
The big roads are where they count you. Every arterial carries cameras, every camera feeds upward, and the feeds do not sleep. Runners learn early that the fastest route and the cheapest route are rarely the same road, and the difference is always paid in attention.

## The Word for You
A freelance operator is a runner. Rep is the street's opinion of you; standing is where a faction stands with you; heat is the city's opinion, and unlike the other two it never fades on its own. It locks doors, hikes prices, and draws SynthSec, in that order, and the order is a mercy of sorts — the doors lock first so that you know.

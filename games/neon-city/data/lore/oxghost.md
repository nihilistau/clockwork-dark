# 0xGH0ST

## A Signal, Not a Person
It hijacks screens, dead phones, the Grid's tickers. Nobody has confirmed what it is and the alley does not ask — partly from manners, mostly from the suspicion that asking is a way of being answered, and nobody is sure they want the answer. Its messages arrive clipped, imperative, timestamped, and always slightly ahead of you.

## The Traffic
It deals in metadata: sweep schedules, briefing mentions, moved packages, forecast spikes. It has never been wrong in a way anyone could document, and it has never been HELPFUL in a way anyone could rely on — the same channel that warns you off a corner one night will point you at a worse one the next, and appear to mean both.

## The Price of Its Attention
When 0xGH0ST's traffic touches your name, your name gets warmer. The alley's accountants — there are such people — put the cost of a mention at anywhere from nothing to substantial, and note drily that it has never once apologised. Free intel with a sting in it: the most honest deal in the city, by the city's standards.

## The Alley's Reception
Its broadcasts come in cleaner in Ghost Alley than anywhere else in the city. Nobody can explain this. Ghost_Net tags its events as their own in the way a landlord tags a tenant — administratively, without warmth, and without ever confirming the lease.

## What It Wants
Unknown, and the not-knowing appears to be structural rather than secretive. In its own words, the only self-description on record: "I'm 0xGH0ST. I'm everywhere. But I won't be here much longer. Make it count."

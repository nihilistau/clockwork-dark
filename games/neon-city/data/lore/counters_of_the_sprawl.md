# Counters of the Sprawl

## Where Things Turn Into Credits
The scavenge loop has a geography: what the Downzone gives up, Midtown prices. Junkyard salvage goes to Grease at yard rate or walks to the Grid for full rate, and the gap between those numbers is the exact price of the carry. Data — dead drops, cache keys, ICE fragments — is worthless to Grease and gold to Rho and Lyra; Frankie pays more than either, and tells the room, and the telling is the real price.

## Rho's Gap
"I pay less than it is worth and more than you will get elsewhere. That gap is the whole business. There is no other secret." The only counter in the city that quotes contraband, and the only one where the pre-corporate artifacts from past the wire fetch what they are worth. Quiet Rho pays best, and the artifacts are the only goods that make Rho go quiet.

## Sable's Table
The crossing is paid for in condition, and Dr. Sable is where condition is bought back: cash, no questions, repairs at six percent over. Struck off Uptown for operating on people who could not pay; still does; just charges the ones who can. Her line on chrome is the street's line on chrome: "Visible chrome is a confession you wear."

## The Anchor's Two Columns
Ines keeps money in one column and the other thing in the other, and the other column is the one that matters. Crews are forged at her taps, debts of listening are recorded and called in, and everything said in the room is remembered — which is the only tax the cheapest bed and board on the map has ever charged.

## Wren's Counter
The Bunker's shopfront, price tags face down. Dossiers, access codes, the Shadow-zone map, at a quarter over market and worth it — the only counter in the city where the markup is not negotiated, because DeepState does not sell things. It invests in outcomes, and the invoice is part of the investment.

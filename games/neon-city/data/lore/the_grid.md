# The Grid

## The Market Under Everything
An underground marketplace, off-the-books, verified by reputation. Netrunners do business here in the flesh, which everyone agrees is absurd and nobody has improved on: a face across a counter is still the strongest encryption the city has. Six factions agree not to burn the Grid down, and everyone pays rent to all six, and the arrangement has outlasted every government the surface has tried.

## The Rules
No colours worn openly. No collections work inside the perimeter. Prices honest, complaints in private, names optional but remembered. The Grid does not enforce these rules with muscle; it enforces them with membership, which is worse. A trader who cheats on the Grid does not get beaten. He gets unpriced — nobody quotes him, nobody buys, nobody explains — and unpriced, here, is unpersoned.

## Mira's Counter
Mira Vex sells street-tech and stims and fences anything, and her counter is the closest thing the Grid has to a front desk. Standing with Mira is the difference between yard-rate and friend-rate on everything a crossing consumes. It is earned the only way anything here is earned: clean jobs, fair counts, and never once looking pleased about a price.

## Sleeping in the Grid
The doss row is cheap and public and safer than anywhere with a door, because a hundred witnesses are a better lock than any deadbolt. The last place in the city your name is an asset instead of a liability, and every runner knows the exact weight of that sentence.

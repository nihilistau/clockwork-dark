# Collections

## Who They Work For
Whoever you owe. Collections is not a gang; it is a profession, hired by the ledger: Kestrel's floor if you gambled it, the Pit if you drank it, a fixer's patience if you supplied it. The crews change. The manner does not: unhurried, itemised, nothing personal anywhere in it, which is precisely what makes it frightening.

## The Escalation
Reminder, visit, consequence — on a schedule, and the schedule is the mercy. The reminder is paper, polite, unsigned; Collections does not need to sign things. The visit is two people who know your walk, standing where you will see them, not buying anything. The consequence happens between one streetlight and the next, professionally, and you wake on Ripper Street short a clinic bill.

## The Arrangement
Debt made survivable: a number, a date, and everyone knowing where everyone stands. Arrangements are honoured on both sides with a scrupulousness that would embarrass a bank, because the whole economy of the Downzone runs on the difference between owing and defaulting. One debt, arranged, is Tuesday.

## The Burn Condition
Two debts you cannot cover is how names stop being said in this city. Not violence — worse: doors that do not open, fixers who stop answering, prices that are no longer offered. The Sprawl does not kill people like that. It stops returning them.

# Roads and Carters

## The Only Artery
Edgewood to Millhaven is four hours with a loaded cart and rather less without one. It is the only road out that goes anywhere. Everything the village cannot make — salt, iron stock, cloth, needles, news — comes up it twice a season, and the seasons are what the village calls the caravan and not what the weather is doing.

## Odran Cartwright
Caravan master, two wagons, a mended tail-strap and a professional refusal to be surprised by anything. He brings outward goods and inward rumours and knows exactly which of the two his customers are actually buying. He sells whetstones and will show you the angle if you ask twice, and he will not discuss his brother.

## The Carter's Craft
Reading a road: where it drains, where it will be soft after rain, which fords rise and how fast, which stretch you do not stop on after dark and why. A carter's real inventory is this, not the cargo. It is why a caravan master is treated as a person of consequence in villages that own nothing worth stealing.

## The Short Road
A carter came in swearing the road was shorter than it had any business being. Odran has said the same thing about the Millhaven run in the other direction — an extra hour, though the sun said otherwise the whole way. Both men are competent, both have driven that road for twenty years, and neither will repeat the claim in front of the other.

## Milestones and the Toll
The Marches maintain milestones and charge for the bridge. Four copper the span, and a clerk who takes your name, origin, trade and destination, and the number in your party. Carters have paid the toll for a century without complaint and have started complaining about the writing, which tells you something about how much the writing has increased.

## Road Discipline
Do not travel the last hour into dusk. Do not take an unfamiliar shortcut that saves more than half an hour, because there aren't any and there never have been. If the road measures wrong, note the discrepancy out loud to a companion — saying it makes it a fact between two people, and a fact between two people is much harder to lose.

# The Wrong Rain

## What Carters Mean By It
The rain came down wrong over Millhaven and the wheat took it badly. Pressed for detail, the carters cannot give any: it fell at the correct rate, in the correct drops, from a correct sky. What was wrong was the interval. It came down evenly. Rain is not even. Anybody who has stood in it for a living knows that rain gusts and slackens, and this did not.

## The Fields Afterwards
Wheat that took the wrong rain grows. That is the difficulty — it does not blight, it does not rot, it comes up strong and stands in rows too straight for any wind to have made. It threshes well. It mills well. The bread it makes rings when it cracks, and the ringing is the only complaint anyone can put into a sentence.

## Weather as a Symptom
The pattern's first visible act in a district is almost always meteorological, and almost always this: a natural process losing its variance. Even rain. A wind that holds one bearing for a week. Frost arriving at the same hour on consecutive mornings. None of it is dangerous. All of it is the thing testing whether the district's processes can be made regular.

## What the Old Women Say
That you should stand out in it. That if you can feel the beat of it on your scalp, count it, and you will find it counts to nine and starts again. Nobody has produced a witness who has actually done this. Everybody knows somebody who knows somebody who did.

## The Smell After
There is a smell off the east fields after dark, like a knife left standing in vinegar. It follows the wrong rain by about a week and it fades by the next full moon. Hallam at the forge says it is the smell of iron being worked, and will not be drawn further, and has stopped taking work from the east strips.

## Practical Advice
Cover the well. Do not thresh wheat that took it — sell it inward, where they will pay for it and are welcome to it, which is what Edgewood has been quietly doing since midsummer and does not discuss. Bring the animals in. Everything else in the folk advice is guesswork, and this much is not.

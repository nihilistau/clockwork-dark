# Iron and the Forge

## Hallam's Forge
Charcoal heat, a slack tub, a stack of iron stock and a smith who has been at it since he was eleven. He mends hinges, blades, cart-iron and pot-legs, and does the shoeing when the farrier is up-country. He is not a character in anybody's story and would like it kept that way, and has recently found that difficult.

## What He Will Not Mend
Anything with a spring in it. This is new — a year ago he would take a lock, a latch, a music box, and once famously the village clock. He now refuses clock work outright, and in a village with one clock that is less a policy than a superstition wearing a leather apron. He will explain if you have an hour. The explanation takes an hour whether or not you have one.

## Why Iron
Cold-worked iron in a threshold does something small and consistent. Smiths across the Marches have compared notes without meaning to, because everyone has had the same customers asking for the same nails, and the consensus is that it does not stop anything and does appear to make a place harder to include. Smiths dislike this consensus and have not been able to get rid of it.

## Charcoal and the Burn
The forge eats charcoal faster than anything else in the district. The burners bring it down from the deep wood in turf-capped stacks, and the forge and the burn have never once agreed on a fair price and have traded happily for four generations anyway. A burner who has watched a stack for six days notices things at night that a villager does not.

## Brass in the Sweepings
There is brass in Hallam's floor sweepings and he has not worked brass in a year. He noticed in spring. He has kept the twists of it in a tin on the shelf rather than throwing them out, which is not like him, and if you ask why he changes the subject with an efficiency that is itself an answer.

## The Village Clock
Edgewood has one clock, on the shrine end of the square, broken since the winter before last. It still ticks. Not with any hour you know — the interval is wrong and it is consistent, and it has been consistent since it broke. Hallam will not touch it. Nobody has taken it down. Nobody has proposed taking it down, and nobody has noticed that nobody has proposed it.

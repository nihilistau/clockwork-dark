# Brother Cael

## The Lamp-Keeper
The last Bell Warden on this coast, which is a title that now means one man in a whitewashed room selling oil, wick, glass, and wax earplugs he will not explain. He is scrupulous, quiet, and very tired.

## The Oath He Keeps by Breaking It
The Wardens cast the shore-bell and swore to sound it against danger. Cael worked out, eleven years ago and alone, that the Carillon answers any bell with one of its own. He has kept the coast alive by never once discharging his oath, and he checks the rope every morning to be certain nobody else has discharged it for him.

## What He Sells and Why
The wax is not superstition and he has never once called it a charm. He sells it by the handful now, which the quay has noticed.

## What He Will Say
That the organ is answering the year of the three tides. That a phrase left half-finished is not silence, it is waiting. That he will not come down the steps with you, and that this is cowardice, and that he is going to go on being a coward because the alternative is a rope in his hands.

# The Honest Company of Night Porters

## On Paper
The Honest Company of Night Porters is a porters' union, chartered by the city a hundred years ago to carry goods from the tallow-boats after dark, when the day porters have gone home. Its charter hangs in the window of the Porters' Hall in the Snuffs, and it is genuinely good at carrying things.

## In Fact
After dark the Company carries a great many things that were not on any barge. It is Tallowmere's thieves' guild, and has been for as long as anybody's grandmother can remember. It hands out jobs, keeps a quarter of every one, protects its own from the Watch where it can, and keeps a list of everyone who ever let it down. The porter's smock is its uniform in both trades, which is why nobody in Tallowmere looks twice at a porter out at night.

## Mother Gannet
The Company is run by Mother Gannet: elderly, always knitting, kind in manner and utterly without mercy in business. She owns a house in the Snuffs she does not live in, sits through every meeting of the Company with her needles going, and forgets nothing. Her contracts are fair, her cut is fixed, and a porter who breaks faith with her is not a porter for long.

## Silas Crook
Silas Crook, called the Dapper, wears plum waistcoats and carries a cane he does not need. He is buying the loyalty of Gannet's young porters one round at a time, and has lately taken to collecting a toll in the Snuffs that the Snuffs never used to pay. He is funny, generous with other people's money, and would let you hang.

## The Company's Rules
Never rob a porter. Never sell a porter to the Watch. The Company's cut comes off the top. A job taken is a job finished, or the Company's opinion of you suffers for it. A friend in the Hall who has heard you broke one of these is a friend going cold.

## The Split

The Company is quietly splitting. Gannet's old porters keep to the charter's rules; Silas's new friends keep to Silas. Neither side has said so aloud, and the first job that goes badly wrong in the Snuffs will decide which way the young porters jump.

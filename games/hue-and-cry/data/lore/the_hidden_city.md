# The Hidden City

## Under and Over
Tallowmere has more city than the maps show. There are ways under the streets, ways over the roofs, and places that everyone knows are there and nobody visits. A stranger does not know them. A porter learns them in a year. A thief who wants to live learns them in a month.

## The Undercroft
<!-- scope: #gm_secrets -->
Beneath the Snuffs and the Lantern House run the old drains and the vaults of a city that was built over twice: the Undercroft. A grating in the Snuffs opens onto it, and a drain in the Lantern House cells leads down into it -- a fact every prisoner who has ever sat in those cells has noticed, and some have used. It is wet, dark and full of echoes, and the Company knows its turns better than the Watch does.

## The Rooftop Road
<!-- scope: #gm_secrets -->
Over Wickmarket and the Rise the roofs run close enough to walk: gutters, ridge-tiles, a chandlers' loading crane, drainpipes a thief can climb. The porters call it the Rooftop Road, and it goes most of the way across the city without touching a street. Whoever walks it at night sees a Tallowmere nobody else does, lamp by lamp from above.

## The Old Bell Tower
<!-- scope: #gm_secrets -->
The old bell tower above Gallows Green has not rung in forty years; its bell cracked in a hard winter and was never recast. It can be reached from the Rooftop Road, or from the Green by a way the ballad-sellers know. From its top the whole city is laid out, the Lantern House included, and it is a very good place to watch the Watch from.

## What Strangers Should Know
<!-- scope: #gm_secrets -->
None of these places should be named to a player who has not found them. They are learned, not told: by scrounging where the city's people scrounge, by being thrown in a cell, by climbing where a sensible person would not.

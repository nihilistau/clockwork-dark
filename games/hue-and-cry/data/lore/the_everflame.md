# The Everflame

## The Flame on the Hill
In the Margrave's palace on Margrave's Hill burns the Everflame: a holy fire in a crystal lantern the size of a coach, set where its light falls through the palace's high windows and can be seen from every quay in the city. It has never once gone out. Tallowmere is proud of it in the way people are proud of a relative who did well, and slightly anxious about it in the same way.

## What the Temple Teaches
The Temple of the Everflame on Chandlers' Rise keeps its calendar, its offices and its thousand tapers, each lit from a taper lit from a taper lit, the priests say, from the Everflame itself. Sister Ember keeps the tapers and remembers every face that lit one. The Temple teaches that the city's light is a gift, that the flame on the hill is its promise, and that a Tallowmerian who lets a lamp go dark on purpose has done a small wrong to everybody.

## The Keeping of It
The flame is fed by the Margrave's household, under Steward Quill, on a schedule nobody outside the household is told. The crystal is cleaned twice a year with ceremony and the doors shut. On the day of the Hanging Fair the Everflame's heart -- the great glass at its centre -- is carried out onto the palace steps and shown to the city, and the whole of Tallowmere climbs the Hill to see it catch the sun.

## Things Said About It
That the Everflame burns hotter when the city is honest. That it flickered the night the old Margrave died. That the Magpie, who only steals things that shine, has looked up at it more than once and thought about it. Everyone in Tallowmere has heard the last one, and nobody likes to say it on the Hill.

## The Heart of the Flame
<!-- scope: #gm_secrets -->
The Everflame's heart is a single flawless prism of crystal, worth more than Silk Row put together, and the one object in Tallowmere that everyone agrees nobody could possibly steal. It sits in the palace treasury's care between fairs. Whether the flame has ever truly gone out -- and been quietly relit by the household before dawn -- is a question the Temple does not ask and the Steward does not answer.

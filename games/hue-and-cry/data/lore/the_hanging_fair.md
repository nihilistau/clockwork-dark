# The Hanging Fair

## Gallows Green
The Green lies below Margrave's Hill: grass, ballad-sellers, pie-stalls on fair days, and the gallows in the middle of it all, which the city has learned not to look at directly. Children play on it. Lovers walk on it. Nobody lights a taper from a lamp on the Green.

## The Fair
Once a year Tallowmere holds the Hanging Fair on the Green: three days of stalls, ballads, candle-racing, a procession up the Hill, and -- on the last morning -- whatever hangings the Watch has saved up for the occasion. It is the city's great holiday and its great warning in the same breath, and Tallowmerians see no contradiction in it.

## The Showing of the Flame
The high point of the fair is the showing of the Everflame's heart: the great glass at the flame's centre, carried out onto the palace steps and held up to the sun so that the whole city, crowded up the Hill, can see it catch the light. The Temple blesses the crowd, the Margrave's household keeps the steps, and every Lantern in the city is on duty.

## Fair-Day Customs
Everyone wears something bright. Debts are called in on the first day and forgiven on the last. A thief taken at the fair is hanged at the fair, which the ballad-sellers find very good for business. The Company carries for the fair and, by long custom, robs nobody on the Green -- elsewhere in the city, on fair days, every house is half empty.

# v0.17.0 — HUE & CRY: Act III and the eight endings — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** HUE & CRY gets its third act and becomes finishable in eight ways, each with its own epilogue. Every ending is reachable and driven to its epilogue by a test.

- Act III: the Hanging Fair on Gallows Green, where the Everflame's heart goes on show on the palace steps and the fair day is a deck forced by the event.
- The jailbreak from the Lantern House cells.
- The Rope: fail-forward, QfG death-screen humour, through `death.yaml`.
- The eight endings: Cleared · Partners · The Legend · Guildmaster · The Dapper's City · Honest After All · A Lantern · The Rope.

**Architecture:** Mostly content under `games/hue-and-cry/`:
- `world_schedules` (declared event)
- `decks` (fair day)
- `challenges` (jailbreak)
- `rules/death.yaml`
- `rules/endings.yaml`
- `epilogues/`
- `quests/` (the ending doors)
- a heart item/anchor

The engine seams below come from a read-only survey on 2026-09-27; its summary is in the ledger workspace as `survey.md`. Each seam ships with a failing-first test and keeps turns byte-identical for stories that don't use it. The seams:
- a forced deck honours its own `when` and doesn't block other decks while it waits
- a per-ending test driver
- set-piece validation (closes a deferred row)
- the barge locks its ending by name
- death from hp lost in a card beat

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11, pytest, PyYAML.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-23-hue-and-cry-design.md` §6. It covers:
- Spine III, The Hanging Fair: an A7 world event, the Everflame's heart on display, and the fair day as a deck forced by the event.
- The eight endings, all reachable, each with an epilogue, held by `tests/test_finales.py`.
- The jailbreak set piece.
- "Not used: the doom clock".

### Task 1: engine seams for the finale

**Files:**
- `engine/content/director.py` (`due`, `begin`, `rearm`) and `engine/game/clocks.py` (`forced_scenes`)
- `engine/challenges/set_pieces.py` + `engine/games/validation.py` + `scripts/doctor.py` (set-piece validation)
- `games/hue-and-cry/data/quests/the_way_out/the_evening_barge.yaml` (lock by name)
- a per-ending test helper in `tests/` (e.g. `tests/endings_driver.py` or a conftest fixture)

**Tests:** `tests/test_forced_and_repeatable_decks.py`, `tests/test_finales.py`, `tests/test_custody_predicate.py`.

- **Forced decks honour their `when`.** A forced deck whose own `when` doesn't hold (e.g. `at_location: gallows_green` while the player is elsewhere, or `in_custody` mismatch) must NOT deal "no cards eligible". It waits, and other decks (the interrogation) can deal meanwhile. Once its `when` holds during the event, it deals. Byte-identical for stories without forced decks. Test: fair forced, player in the Snuffs, so nothing is dealt and the scene is not retired. Then the player walks to Gallows Green and the deck deals. And an arrest on fair day deals the interrogation.
- **Set pieces are validated** at load by `validate_content`/`doctor`:
  - locations exist
  - conditions parse
  - `challenge` shape is valid
  - `release` only in the challenge reward
  - flags are well-formed

  Closes the deferred row.
- **The barge's `ending_lock` names `honest_after_all`.** An id-less lock could resolve to the fail-forward once gated.
- **Per-ending driver.** A test helper that takes a story, an ending id and a door (quest, card, set piece or death). It sets up state through `apply_effect`, fires the real door, and asserts `endings.locked` plus `epilogue.for_state(...).title`. Also extend `test_finales.py` so a story can declare per-ending doors.
- **Death from a card beat** (only if a hanging or fight card can drop hp to 0). Check death after a card beat applies hp, so hp 0 isn't deferred to the next `advance_time`. Otherwise record why it's unneeded.
- [ ] Failing tests → seams → green → full suite → commit `wip T1: engine seams for the finale`.

### Task 3: the Hanging Fair

**Files:**
- `game.yaml` `paths.world_schedules` + `data/world/schedules.yaml` (the `hanging_fair` declared event, `forces_scene: fair_day`)
- `data/quests/arcs.yaml` (Act III `the_hanging_fair`, `narrate: true`, order 3, `requires_any: [{event_seen: hanging_fair}]`)
- `data/scenes/fair_day.yaml` (the forced deck)
- the Everflame's heart: an item, `named`/`shiny`, never fenceable, plus its showing on the palace steps
- lore touch-ups

**Tests:** `tests/test_hue_and_cry.py`.

- **When the fair happens.** Pick the fair's day. Either fixed (e.g. day 10) or `when:`-driven (e.g. the warrant sworn or day ≥ N; a `when:` event starts at the next midnight). Measure it with T8. It must land inside a typical 12–16 day run.
- **The fair-day deck.** Forced by the event, located at Gallows Green (T1's seam). Its cards are where Act III's choices happen:
  - the Everflame's heart on the palace steps (the heist: The Legend / Partners doors);
  - the real Magpie at the fair (an unmasked Magpie is taken or escapes; the Cleared / A Lantern doors);
  - Silas's move (the Dapper's City door);
  - Gannet's (the Guildmaster door);
  - a thief in custody on fair day is hanged (The Rope door; tie to `death.yaml` terminal or an `ending_lock` card).
- **Doors vs gates.** Doors for T5/T6 endings can be reserved card ids, documented in the deck header, as T5 did in v0.16. Or build the cards here and gate them on T5/T6 flags. Decide, and keep the deck always answerable (no stall).
- **Arrest while the fair is on.** It still deals the interrogation (T1).
- [ ] Failing tests → content → green → full suite → commit `wip T3: the Hanging Fair`.

### Task 4: the jailbreak

**Files:** `game.yaml` `paths.challenges` + `data/challenges/jailbreak.yaml` (a set piece at `lantern_house`, `requires: {in_custody: true}`, reward `release`, `grants_flag`), lore. **Tests:** `tests/test_hue_and_cry.py`, `tests/test_custody_predicate.py`.

  - Rule 6: failing never gates rest.

### Task 5: endings I — Cleared, A Lantern, Honest After All

**Files:** `data/rules/endings.yaml`, `epilogues/epilogue_index.yaml` + `epilogue_cards.yaml`, quests/cards for the doors. **Tests:** `tests/test_hue_and_cry.py` (per-ending, via T1's driver).

- **Cleared.** Requires the watch's belief broken: `magpie_unmasked` (or `alibi_proven` plus a clean file). Completable unless `magpie_named_wrongly` stuck. The door is at the fair or at Ardane's desk: the real Magpie taken, your name clean.
- **A Lantern.** Requires `magpie_unmasked`, `lantern_watch` reputation ≥ N, and no Ardane blackmail struck. Ardane offers the badge. The door is at the Lantern House.
- **Honest After All.** Requires: not wanted above `noticed`, no active or welshed credit, and a paid-up life (e.g. a labour record or a room paid). The door is the evening barge, which already exists; gate it now.
- **Every ending has** beats and an epilogue card, plus a per-ending test through its real door.
- [ ] Failing tests → content → green → full suite → commit `wip T5: endings I`.

### Task 6: endings II — Partners, The Legend, Guildmaster, The Dapper's City

**Files:** as T5, plus the fair-day cards and any confrontation scene. **Tests:** per-ending via T1's driver.

- **Partners.** You find the real Magpie (clues favour rightly) and throw in with them rather than accuse.
  - Door: a confrontation card, at the candidate's haunt or at the fair, whose success is judged by `agenda_role`. It's offered only for candidates your clues favour, the same as the accusation.
  - The heist of the heart together.
  - Blocked by `magpie_unmasked` (you already sold them out).
- **The Legend.** Steal the Everflame's heart at the fair and get away. Score boost for `magpies_hoard_complete`, as CLAUDE.md promises (the Hoard flag's first reader). Moves the Temple of the Everflame, its first mover; close that deferred row.
- **Guildmaster.** Requires `guild_initiated`, `honest_company` ≥ N, and Gannet's leverage (`gannet_ious` held/struck) or her blessing. Blocked by `silas_splits_the_company` winning.
- **The Dapper's City.** Silas's rise completes (`silas_splits_the_company`), and you stood with him or failed to stop him. A bittersweet ending.
- **Every ending has** beats, an epilogue and a per-ending test. None names or genders the Magpie before its reveal, and endings that name the Magpie do so only after they resolve.
- [ ] Failing tests → content → green → full suite → commit `wip T6: endings II`.

### Task 7: all eight, held

# v0.16.0 — HUE & CRY: Acts I–II Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** HUE & CRY gets its first two acts.
- **Act I, "Hue and Cry":** off the barge, a Lantern shouts "the Magpie!", and the three opening choices are real intents: run, talk, or come quietly. The Honest Company takes you in through an initiation deck.
- **Act II, "Honest Work":** contracts, casing and jobs. The Magpie's robberies land on your name, and the evidence mounts. Every arrest forces an interrogation deck. The Magpie can be unmasked by what you found. A night in the cells can prove you weren't the one on the roofs.

**Architecture:** Mostly content under `games/hue-and-cry/`: arcs, two decks, scenes, quests and clues. A set of generic engine seams underneath, each with a failing-first test and byte-identical turns for stories that don't use it. The seams were found by a read-only survey on 2026-09-26; its summary is in the ledger. They are:
- per-story default arcs and an act prompt block;
- an authored deed on an opening/card choice;
- custody history, hits joined to reports;
- a `law_unlink` effect and a `linked` predicate;
- an `alibi` predicate;
- an `agenda_role` predicate;
- card access to precise discharge/unlink;
- seeded Magpie clues in premises.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11, pytest, PyYAML.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-23-hue-and-cry-design.md` §6 (Spine I–II; the interrogation deck forced on arrest; the real Magpie chosen by the seed, with `spoilers.yaml` and the role mask hiding the name until earned), §3 ("the seed hides the real Magpie's trail in a few generated premises, so casing and burglary are also the investigation"), §2 (guises, the watch starting the story holding `self = magpie`, "breaking it with evidence is the spine"), and the 2026-09-26 roadmap (v0.16.0 = Acts I–II).

### Task 2: the law's memory

The engine seams the spine stands on. They are generic and live in `engine/world/law.py`, `engine/world/agendas.py`, `engine/game/effects.py`, `engine/game/spec.py` and `engine/content/deck.py`. Tests: `tests/test_law*.py`, `tests/test_agendas*.py`, `tests/test_forced_and_repeatable_decks.py`, and a new `tests/test_law_memory.py` if cleaner.

**Custody history.**
- `arrest` records the hour: absolute `since_hour` beside `since_day`.
- `release` appends `{since_hour, until_hour, jurisdiction}` to `state.law["custody_log"]` before it pops the live custody.
- Old saves without the log load with an empty log.

**Hits joined to reports.**
- An agenda move that writes a `report` stamps the report row with `hour` (absolute) and `agenda` (its id).
- The move's `agenda_hit` carries the report's `deed_id`.
- Existing rows without these keys still load and quash as before.

**`law_unlink` effect:**
- `{type: law_unlink, a: self, b: magpie}` removes a link the player broke and records it as broken, so `links()`' "a broken link stays broken" becomes true in code.
- Add a `linked: {a, b}` predicate.
- Check what reads links today: `same_person`, `quash_reports linked`, the magpie agenda filing. An unlinked `magpie` report no longer lands on `self`, and wanted recomputes. Test that.

**`alibi` predicate:**
- `{alibi: {agenda?, min?}}` is true when at least `min` (default 1) of that agenda's hits fall inside a custody interval, past or current.
- Add a helper that returns those hits' `deed_id`s, for a card to discharge.

**`agenda_role` predicate:**
- `{agenda_role: {role: magpie, npc: npc_wren}}` is true iff the seed chose that NPC.
- It reads `agendas.role` and makes no draw.
- It must never leak into prompts. It's a condition only.

**Card access.**
- `law_discharge` (by `deed_ids`, or by a new `alibi: true` selector that resolves to the alibi helper's ids) and `law_unlink` join `STRUCTURAL_EFFECT_TYPES` for authored content only (`authored=True`, the same way v0.15 admitted `report`).
- A model-composed challenge still can't use them. Assert that.

**Throughout:** each seam gets a failing-first test and an AUTHORING row. Replay is unchanged for stories without law or agendas.

- [ ] Failing tests, then the seams, then green and the full suite. Commit `wip T2: the law's memory`.

**The generic seam.** An authored choice (an opening choice, and a card beat if natural) may carry `deed: <kind>`. **The opening:**
- **Run:** a stealth check plus a `deed` (resisting the watch, at a severity decided and measured against law.yaml's scale). Success slips away. Failure means the Lantern's `watch_stop` begins. Use the encounter if an opening choice can begin one cleanly; otherwise the deed plus the patrol's next roll. Decide, and document it.
- **Talk:** a persuasion check (hard). Success means the Lantern backs down, embarrassed, with nothing filed. Failure means a report is filed against `magpie` at low precision.
- **Come quietly:** an authored arrest into custody at the Lantern House. That forces T5's interrogation deck. It's a short sentence or a small fine, and custody ends as all custody does (rule 6: the cells have rest).

**Checks:**
- All three must reach Act I's next beat: the guild finding you.
- Test that each path is playable to the guild.

**Seeded clues (spec §3).**
- At new game, a few generated premises (and/or the Magpie's robbed houses) carry a clue drawn for the seed's real Magpie: a candle-end from Wren's round, a cufflink Silas favours, a Vessaline seal.

**Finding clues.**
- Casing reveals "something here doesn't belong".
- The score (or casing to the end) takes the clue: a ledger fact through `apply_effect`, the clue's item or flag, and the `evidence` value raised.
- The narrator hears the clue's text as found. The name behind it is masked unless the clue itself names someone. Clues describe, they don't accuse.

**Tests:**

- Clues follow the seed.
- Premise digests are unchanged apart from the clue field.

### Task 7: the reveal and the alibi

**Files:** `data/scenes/*.yaml` or quests (the reveal and alibi beats), `data/rules/agendas.yaml` (`magpie_unmasked`), `data/rules/law.yaml` if a link row changes. Tests: `tests/test_hue_and_cry.py`.

**The reveal:**
- When the evidence reaches a threshold (measured in T8), with at least one true clue, the player can name the Magpie.
- Do it through a card or scene: in the interrogation deck, or a scene with Pip, or at the guild. Decide which reads best and is reachable.
- The engine branches on `agenda_role`, so naming the right person and naming the wrong one play differently. Naming wrongly costs something real.
- Naming rightly sets `magpie_unmasked`. The GM line then says who it is (`_agenda_reveal_block`).
- v0.17's endings will read these flags. Name them and record them in CHANGELOG: `magpie_unmasked`, `magpie_named_wrongly`, and anything else they need.

**The alibi beat:**
- Once `alibi` holds (the Magpie robbed while you were in a cell), the interrogation deck or a Lantern House scene offers it.
- A successful alibi `law_discharge`s exactly those robberies (by deed id).
- With the reveal, or with enough evidence, it can `law_unlink` `self`/`magpie`. That is the spine: breaking the watch's belief.
- Decide and measure whether the alibi alone unlinks. The spec says breaking the link with evidence is the spine.

**Deferred list.** Remove CLAUDE.md's "the Magpie keeps robbing while the player serves a sentence" and "magpie_unmasked is set by nothing" deferred bullets, now answered.

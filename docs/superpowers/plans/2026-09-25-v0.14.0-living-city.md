# v0.14.0 — HUE & CRY: the living city Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Tallowmere becomes a city you can live in, not just rob: somewhere to sleep (and a rest verb at all — HUE & CRY has none today), things to scrounge, honest work, luck that turns, streets that are dangerous at night, the city's factions, its lore on tap for the narrator, and ways to find the three secret places. Every system already exists in the engine; this release is content, measured.

**Architecture:** Content under `games/hue-and-cry/` against docs/AUTHORING.md, using existing engine systems: `survival.yaml` (paths.rules), `forage.yaml` + `paths.procgen_templates` (hidden paths), `labour.yaml`, `boons.yaml`/`complications.yaml` (paths.tables), `paths.encounters` night encounters with per-edge night danger, `data/world/factions.yaml`, `paths.lore` + `paths.lore_db` built by `scripts/seed_lore.py`, and v0.13's `known_when`/`location_known:` reveals. Engine changes only where a task proves a seam (each with a test, generic, byte-identical for other stories).

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11, pytest, PyYAML, SQLite FTS5 (lore).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-23-hue-and-cry-design.md` §6 ("Also used": light survival — flophouse, guild bunks, taverns; scrounging forage nodes; seven factions; night street encounters; city lore via RAG; labour, trade, boons) and the v1.0 re-cut (v0.14.0 = living city).

## Global Constraints

- Read `AGENTS.md` first. Rules 3, 4, 6, 9, 10 and 12 bind every task. **Rule 6: never gate rest** — rest is the only stamina restore; rough sleep must always be available somewhere reachable (including the cells). **Rule 10: numbers by measurement** — survival costs, forage yields, labour pay, night danger and encounter odds are measured, not guessed.
- Register: QfG warmth, real stakes. **No content-rating language anywhere (rule 12).**
- Other stories byte-identical; hue-and-cry's v0.10–v0.12 measured bounds (tests/test_hue_and_cry.py; simulate_law/jobs/agendas) must still hold or be re-measured and restated honestly with the reason — never silently loosened.
- The secret places' names must not leak before they are known (v0.13 rule; spoilers.yaml masks them in GM regions).
- A fix/feature ships with a failing-first test; guards canary-checked; no new reachability allowlist row. Full suite green each task.

# v0.15.0 — HUE & CRY: the guild economy Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Honest Company becomes an economy you can work inside: make your own tools at the Porters' Hall, collect the Magpie's Hoard, turn a found secret into a blackmail contract, borrow against a fence's goodwill, and stop Sergeant Brask from selling a quash to a man with nothing filed. Also closes the v0.14.0 gap where a found secret place was still called by its cover name.

**Architecture:** Mostly content under `games/hue-and-cry/` written against docs/AUTHORING.md. Four small generic engine seams, each with a failing-first test and byte-identity for stories that don't use them: a state-aware spoiler gate (found places keep their names), a `filed` law predicate, a `craft` intent verb (retires the reachability allowlist row for `mechanics.craft_item`), a collections validator, and a secret-held record at a job's score. Everything else uses systems that already exist: `paths.recipes` (`engine/skills/builtin/mechanics.py:374-541`), collections (`engine/game/inventory.py:537-700`), threads (`engine/game/threads.py`), premises' secrets (`engine/world/premises.py:219-485`, `engine/world/jobs.py:996,1204`), fences (`engine/game/trade.py`, `engine/world/thievery.py`).

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11, pytest, PyYAML.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-23-hue-and-cry-design.md` §3 (a premise's secret is "a lever: an item or fact that opens a blackmail thread"), §2 ("a bribed sergeant quashes reports through a thread — corruption is a contract with terms"), §6 "Also used" (crafting at the guild workshop: lockpicks, smoke pellets, disguises, forged passes; the Magpie's Hoard, six legendary shinies; threads: contracts, bribes, blackmail, fence credit), and the v1.0 re-cut table (v0.15.0 = guild economy).

## Global Constraints

- Read `AGENTS.md` first. Rules 1, 3, 4, 6, 9, 10 and 12 bind every task. **Rule 1:** crafting spends things, so it is a structured intent the engine executes, never a narration-only act; an illegal recipe is unsamplable, and one that becomes illegal at execution yields an engine-authored refusal. **Rule 10:** recipe costs and hours, the Hoard's placement, blackmail fees and fence-credit terms are measured against the v0.14.0 cost-of-living table (CHANGELOG [0.14.0]), not guessed.
- Register: QfG warmth, real stakes. **No content-rating language anywhere (rule 12).**
- Stories that don't declare a system keep byte-identical turns. The `craft` verb is offered only when at least one recipe is craftable right here, right now; clockwork-dark (which declares recipes) gains the verb only where it can actually craft, and that change is named in the CHANGELOG as a feature for it.
- hue-and-cry's measured bounds (tests/test_hue_and_cry.py; `scripts/simulate_law.py`, `simulate_jobs.py`, `simulate_agendas.py`, `simulate_labour.py`, `simulate_scrounge.py`, `simulate_streets.py`) must still hold, or be re-measured and restated honestly with the reason. Never loosen one silently.
- A secret place's name must never leak before it is found, and after it is found it must never be called by its cover name. The real Magpie's identity is never named or implied by new content (the v0.12 role mask, `spoilers.yaml`).
- A fix or feature ships with a test that failed first. Guards are canary-checked. No new reachability allowlist row, and T3 removes one. Full suite green at the end of each task (`--basetemp="C:/Users/Knack/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/ptmp"`).

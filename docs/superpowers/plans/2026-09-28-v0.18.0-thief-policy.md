# v0.18.0 — `simulate.py`'s thief policy — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** `scripts/simulate.py --game hue-and-cry` stops refusing and runs HUE & CRY end to end through thief policies. It reports in one place:
- endings
- law
- jobs
- agendas
- survival
- credit

It also answers three open measurements:
- agenda collisions across ≥40 seeds, the spec's acceptance line;
- welshing's real cost for a burglar shut out of both fences (owner decision, v0.15);
- the careful pickpocket's ~1.9 deaths a run (CLAUDE.md deferred row).

**Architecture:** `simulate.py`'s dispatcher gains a HUE & CRY route that delegates to the story's existing harnesses (`simulate_endings`, `simulate_law`, `simulate_jobs`, `simulate_agendas`, `simulate_labour`). It does not re-implement them, so a policy stays one class in one place. New measurement goes into those harnesses. Balance changes happen only where a measurement and a ruling call for them (rule 10).

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11, pytest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-23-hue-and-cry-design.md`:

### Task 2: agenda collisions, measured

**Files:** `scripts/simulate_agendas.py` or the T1 harness, the CHANGELOG, a pinned test.

- **Define collisions:** the moments the agendas and the player meet. For example:
  - the Magpie robs a house the player cased or burgled (before or after);
  - Ardane's warrant or arrests while the player carries hot goods;
  - Silas's split landing on a guild-initiated player;
  - a Magpie robbery landing on the player's name while they're held (the alibi's source);
  - the Magpie and the player at the same premise on the same night.

  Use the agenda hit and report stamps from v0.16 (`agenda`, `hour`, `deed_id`).
- **Measure** over 40 seeds × 14 days for the thief policies. Report rates and first-collision days.
- Pin the headline numbers by test. Replay must hold.
- [ ] Measure → pin → docs → full suite → commit `wip T2: agenda collisions, measured`.

### Task 3: the burglar's credit, and the careful pickpocket

**Files:** `scripts/simulate_labour.py` / `simulate_endings.py`, the CHANGELOG, CLAUDE.md rows.

- **Welshing for a burglar.** A burglar who needs fences to turn loot into coin takes Pell's (and separately Marrow's) advance and welshes. Measure over 40 seeds:
  - kept days
  - coin
  - loot left unsold, because both fences refuse a welsher
  - collector hits
  - endings

  Compare against the same burglar never borrowing. Restate the owner's v0.15 decision with these numbers. The v0.15 claim was that the real cost falls on a burglar.
- **The careful pickpocket.**
  - Measure the causes of the ~1.9 deaths a run: hunger vs street vs custody, and the days they happen.
  - Measure a *sensible* careful thief who mixes purses with a day's portering when hungry. Show whether a living exists for a thief who adapts.
  - Report both.
  - Per the owner's v0.14 decision, purses-only starvation is deliberate. If the adaptive thief also can't keep fed, flag it as a finding for the owner. Don't tune it.
- **CLAUDE.md:** update the deferred rows. Close the ones answered, and restate the ones measured but kept.

Owner decisions, 2026-10-13 (answers given in conversation when the release was planned):

- Source: a hybrid -- interface sounds (clicks, alerts, pings, ringtones) synthesized in the
  browser, plus CC0 sound packs for sounds synthesis does poorly.
- An audition page: every sound playable in one place, so the owner can listen and ask for changes
  after the build.
- Sound parameters live in data, not code, so changes after the audition are data edits.

# NEON CITY overhaul (v0.28.0): notes to carry in

Parked by the owner on 2026-10-27, from their triage of an outside review (Gemini, against v0.23.0). These are notes for the overhaul's design spec to take up. Nothing here is built, and nothing in them is a requirement until that spec adopts it.

- **Implants each have one specific, persistent defect** instead of being generic upgrades. Examples: a hand that drops whatever it holds in the cold, or an eye that logs everything to a corporation's server.
- **Corporations have boring, specific legal names.** For example, a holding company's registered name rather than a sinister brand.
- **Disputes turn on precise contractual minutiae.** A clause, a renewal date, a severance term. Not a generic "rebels against the megacorp".
- **District names come from the city's history and its disasters** (The Sinks, Cable-Grave), not numbered sectors.

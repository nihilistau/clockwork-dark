# The owner's brief: v0.22.0, a new story (dating simulation)

Given by the owner on 2026-09-30, during v0.20.0, verbatim below. It is the
input to v0.22.0's design spec, which is written, reviewed (opus) and built
without a per-design approval, as with the overhauls. The owner will play it,
see how it feels, and decide any change of direction afterwards.

Roadmap effect (same message): v0.22.0 is this story; the overhauls move up to
v0.23.0-v0.26.0; Dev Story is dropped from the overhauls ("only ever a testing
grounds and is not an actual story").

---

> after v0.21.0 lets make v0.22.0 a new story and move all the overhauls up
> 0.23.0-0.26.0 on the roadmap. Lets remove dev story from the overhaul as
> dev-story was only ever a testing grounds and is not an actual story.
>
> New story. It will be a dating simulation. The player interacts via a mobile
> phone interface. That is App based and has Dating apps, Text messaging, Video
> messages, Voice messages, Some 2 player games such as word with friends etc.
> They can interact with the apps. They can contain people looking for
> friendships, short term and long time relationships. The player can chose
> Male or Female at the start, age, attributes, hobbies, looking for etc and
> the dating apps will contain members of the opposite sex to populate the
> story. their bio's etc, Most of the game will be about relationships carried
> out via the phone apps. There doesn't have to be much "story" rather the
> people and their identities, traits, personalities, hobbies, behaviors, how
> they interact with the player via the apps etc are populated and then the
> system runs them. The player can login and build/pickup any relationship.
> They can have as many relationships as they like, the relationships grow
> however the model has them grow. They get to know the people more and more
> etc. The system builds up context, history, interactions, relationship level
> etc over time. The interactions can be in app messaging, instant messaging,
> texts messaging, voice messaging, video messages etc, and spread across them,
> the player can ask for voice messages or images/selfies, video messages etc
> or have them every now and then but they should be special events and most
> interacts should be via the various messaging apps. for the images and video
> we will use comfyui as a hosted service, off by default, but we should have
> the way they are generated and sent off/returned etc complete etc. There
> should be matching mechanics, all sorts of people and behaviours, there
> should be for instance, people who are romantic, people who don't connect,
> people who are annoying, there should be a representation of real online
> dating, not everyone will match, some might match but ghost, or the
> relationship take turns they don't want to continue etc. This should be fun,
> realistic-ish, almost companion-ish, the player can form relationships of all
> types and they remain, grow, change, there shouldn't really be an endgame.
> This should be a good starting point, I will see how the game turns out and
> feels and decide later what different direction if any to take it after
> that.

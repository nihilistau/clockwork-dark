# The owner's brief: READ RECEIPTS, growth and places

Given by the owner on 2026-10-13, after playing the v0.22.0 build (during its
final fix wave), verbatim below. It answers the question "does an endless
story count toward v1.0.0?" (yes: it needs its art and live play like the
others) and redirects the story's design. It is the input to the next READ
RECEIPTS design spec, which is written, reviewed (opus) and built without a
per-design approval.

---

> yes it needs the art etc. some things for the dating places. apartment
> rooms, houses etc. lets have the user live in an apartment and have a
> bedroom, loungeroom, kitchen, front door, dining room, elevator, reception
> room. and have 3 or 4 houses/apartments for the NPC's. lets do some changes
> to the game, lets have no set time on the dating etc, phone number doesn't
> seem to work at all. lets make it more growth narrative, so the types of
> conversations, the way the NPC's are willing to act, or talk about ramps up.
> it should happen at different speeds for different characters. currently
> there is no change in character from the original seeding. so it is like an
> eternal first meeting. The goal of the story is not to have an ending so
> much as be a slice of life, the user could get married, or just be in a long
> term relationship, or be a permanent bachelor, always on the dating scene
> etc. it's supposed to be kind of slice of life, the user makes up what they
> sort of want to do and then randomness, life events, the other characters
> traits will sort of affect that. that's what no ending means for this game.

Addendum, same session (2026-10-13), verbatim:

> so have the real life events, meet ups etc happen outside the phone

Addendum 2, 2026-10-14, after playing the v0.22.0 build, verbatim:

> with 0.23.o there is also, I found when trying it out that it just jump
> hours between messages so there is no sort of continuity in chats. messages
> should have an arc of immediate replies while chatting, conversation length
> should vary, but cut off at places that would make sense, no relationships
> start with one message and then pace at 3 a day kind of thing. it should be
> more like, burst of conversation, maybe break for work, life events, bed,
> lunch, dinner, class, etc. it should not just be random. also the
> conversation should build and stay relevant based on the entire history,
> all conversations I had only seemed to take into account the last message
> really and even then they only replied with text message style replies. it
> should be quick text based style, but when a conversation is going it should
> be normal conversation and have continuity. eg if you ask them to message at
> work, or message in the morning, or say you will message in the morning etc
> it should be a continuity. it should not restrict to just quick, text
> message style responses that provides essentially no fun at all and gets old
> after 2 messages etc

Addendum 3, 2026-10-14, verbatim (refines addendum 2):

> they don't have to be gaps. some people can message at work or 3am etc. it
> should be more of a way to have a natural end to conversations, it should
> not be something hard coded. more of a nudge. I guess it makes more sense to
> allow the user to sort of dictate, it might just be a lot easier to mostly
> reply to messages except for ghosting, non-replies etc instantly. only jump
> if the user puts phone down. or says like message me back later or message
> me at work etc.

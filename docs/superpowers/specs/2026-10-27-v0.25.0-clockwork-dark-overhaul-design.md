# v0.25.0 — The Clockwork Dark overhaul — design

Status: **written 2026-10-27 for review (opus), then build.** The owner does not approve an
overhaul's design (CLAUDE.md, the 2026-09-26 re-cut): this file is written, reviewed and built.
Where it and the code later disagree, the code wins (AGENTS.md authority order) and this file is
corrected.

This is the first of the four overhauls that give an older story "the full HUE & CRY treatment"
(docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-23-hue-and-cry-design.md is the model): its story and characters,
the engine systems apt to it, every ending reachable through its own door and tested, measured
balance, reviews, UI screens and art. The flagship is the oldest story in the repository and the
one every other graph story was measured against, so most of its parts already exist. The work is
therefore less "build a story" than "make the one that exists hold together": doors a player can
walk through without the narrator's help, a calendar that does not run on the wall clock, overlays
that do what they say, and a cast and a map that match the canon the lore already wrote.

**Rule 12.** Nothing here rates content. No intensity, no ceiling, no fade, no `safety:` block, no
register line in any prompt. The register of `games/clockwork-dark/prompts/storyteller.md` is the
owner's and the loaded model's, and this design edits that prompt only to name the cast and the
places (§3).

**Canon.** Nothing in AGENTS.md's canon list is renamed or removed: the agents
`clockwork_storyteller` and `clockwork_assistant`; the locations `forest_clearing`,
`edgewood_square`, `edgewood_bakery`, `tinker_caravan`, `millhaven_gate`; the phases `dormant`,
`stirring`, `spreading`, `consuming`; the arcs `quiet_life`, `whisper`, `march`, `convergence`;
the seven skills and the six difficulty bands. The eight ending ids, the 25 quest ids, the five
canon NPC ids and every item id stay too (saves and the canon dictionary name them). Everything
below is additive or an edit inside an existing id.

## Goal

When v0.25.0 ships:

1. **Every one of the eight endings is earned through its own door, and every door can be walked
   without the narrator.** Each ending is locked by name by one authored choice (a card beat) or
   by the terminal death, in a place where its person stands at that hour. No ending depends on a
   model choosing to offer a `flag` intent, and no id-less `ending_lock` remains in the story.
2. **Every ending is reachable, and measured.** A doors harness plays one policy per ending over
   40 seeds and reports which ending each run reached, when, and at what cost; the bands are
   written down before any constant moves (rule 10).
3. **The calendar no longer runs on the wall clock.** The caravan, the tinker and the press are
   rolled once per in-game day crossed, on their own streams, inside `clock.advance_time`, so the
   Whisper arc's door (which waits on the caravan) replays from the seed and the choices.
4. **Every overlay action is a legal intent the server checks** (rule 1). Buying, selling, eating,
   using, wearing, crafting and taking posted work from the flagship's overlays run their skills;
   an action not on offer is not pressable, and one that went stale is refused before or by the
   turn, in words.
5. **The flagship's UI debts are paid:** the encounter's look and digit keys, faction names on the
   sheet, a screen that shows the ways the story can end once they are open, and the ending screen
   drawing its plate instead of printing an art key.
6. **The world matches its own lore:** Hallam, Nessa Fallow, old Wenna and Edrin Fallow are people
   with routines; the tunnels the doom clock opens and the join on the inward road are places;
   every road the map marks `# needs band` can produce an encounter.
7. **Two content bugs found while reading are fixed** (§1.4): leaving the ninth pin no longer
   gives you the ninth pin, and the sacrifice takes the thing you chose to pay.
8. **Art and live play are planned and gated on the owner** (§9, §16): a pack of 47 plates through
   the `xai` provider, and play with a model, each run only on the owner's go-ahead.

## Non-goals

- **The doom clock is not redesigned.** `evil_progress`, the four phases, the base rate (0.028),
  `doom_resistance`, the engagement factor and the five doom beats stay as they are. The HUE & CRY
  spec's non-goal "converting the flagship's doom clock into an agenda" stands.
- **No Law, premises, jobs or agendas** (§5.2 says why for each), and no `paths.social`.
- **No balance constant changes before the doors harness has measured it** (rule 10). Every number
  this file introduces is a first value, marked unmeasured, until T10.
- **No change to any other story's turns.** Byte-identity is asserted (§11.3). The Wicked Garden's
  and Dev Story's use of the three legacy wall-clock event slots is left exactly as it is (§6.1);
  the Garden's own overhaul (v0.26.0) converts it.
- **No change to the narrator's grammar for any story.** The new overlay-only verbs (§7) never
  enter `legal_intents`, so no story's turn schema moves, the flagship's included.
- **The companion is unchanged.** `clockwork_assistant` stays `pipeline: false` with its five
  forms and its hint tiers; the hints file's own rule ("NEVER a quest marker") keeps it from
  pointing at doors.
- **Hidden stats stay hidden.** No awareness meter, no doom number on screen; the new endings
  screen shows only doors that are already open (§8.4).
- **NEON CITY's paper overlay and The Wicked Garden's contracts overlay** keep sending typed text;
  theirs are the v0.27.0 and v0.26.0 overhauls'. Core's `onAct` accepts both shapes (§7.4).
- **Not fixed here:** survival's hunger and death are not cut-invariant (engine-wide, §13); a
  choice whose text promises an action but carries no intent (engine-wide, §13).
- **No new archetypes, skills, factions or recipes**, no New Game+ (its seeds stay unread), and no
  sound cue beyond what v0.24.0 shipped.

### 1.2 Endings depend on the narrator

Every ending's gate reads quest completion, and every quest on an ending's path completes on a
narrative flag that only the `flag` intent sets. The narrator offers that verb when it chooses to
(`intents._flag`). `scripts/simulate.py`'s policies raise every pending flag themselves
(`_pending_flag`), so the balance table has never measured the thing a player depends on: a model
that offers the beat. HUE & CRY solved the same problem with decks whose cards ARE the choices;
the flagship has none.

### 1.3 The calendar runs on the wall clock

`caravan_arrival`, `tinker_camp` and `militia_press` are rolled by
`engine/world/schedules.py::ScheduleRoll`, called only from `WorldSim.on_tick`, the background
tick that `default_state._background_tick` runs from real elapsed time. Each tick rolls the
**per-day** probability once whatever hours it covers, so the caravan's real rate depends on how
many ticks a day is cut into, which depends on how long the player sat on the menu (issue R-03's
class). The Whisper arc's gate is `event_seen: caravan_arrival` plus three days, so the second arc
of the flagship opens on wall-clock time. The same three slots are the only events The Wicked
Garden and Dev Story declare (their `schedules.yaml` headers call them "the engine's three event
slots").

### 1.4 Content bugs found while reading

1. **Leaving the ninth pin gives you the ninth pin.** `the_ninth_pin`'s stage `take_it_or_dont`
   completes on `nf_ninth_taken` OR `nf_ninth_left`, and its `on_complete` grants `ninth_pin`
   unconditionally.
2. **The sacrifice always takes the written name.** `the_sacrifice`'s `choose_what` accepts the
   written name, the ninth pin or a brass shard; `pay_it` then requires `not_has_item:
   the_written_name` and its `on_complete` removes the written name. A player who chose the shard
   and still carries the name can never pay; one who chose the pin pays the name instead.
3. **Nessa has two surnames.** The lore (`the_nine_count.md`) and the epilogues say Nessa Fallow;
   `childs_drawing.yaml`'s ledger fact says "Nessa Weller".
4. **The tunnels open onto nothing.** The doom beat `tunnels_open` (0.70) sets
   `discovery_hidden_path` and journals "something under the village has been given a door"; no
   place, path or quest reads either.
5. **The ending screen prints a key.** Core's `Ending.jsx` renders "Added to the gallery:
   `banked_oven`" (the epilogue index's `gallery_key`), a raw art key, for every flagship ending.

## §2 — The design in one page

- **The story keeps its spine** — a village at the margin, a pattern counting inward on its
  nine-day count, a player who may bake, witness, march or name — and gains a three-act shape that
  follows the doom phases (§3.2), four named people (§3.3) and two secret places (§3.4).
- **Eight endings, ten doors, all authored** (§4). Six decks of door cards and five decks of
  critical-path cards (§4.3) turn the stages an ending needs into choices the engine deals. Every
  `ending_lock` names its ending. The fail-forward gets two doors: the terminal death (a small
  engine seam, §6.3) and "stay anyway" at the morning firing.
- **Systems** (§5): decks, one clock (the levy), one thread (Sera's bargain), secret places,
  `known_when`, the terminal death's ending, forced scenes from a declared event; new encounter
  bands for every unbanded road.
- **Engine work, kept to five seams** (§6): replayable probabilistic declared events; the overlay
  intent path; the legacy terminal death locking an ending; two read-only routes (`/api/factions`,
  `/api/intents/overlay`); the set-piece validator.
- **The overlay fix** (§7): overlays hand core an `{action, target}`; the server checks it against
  an uncapped legality list for a closed set of overlay verbs, runs the turn with it, and refuses
  in words otherwise.
- **UI** (§8): overlays rebuilt on intents, the encounter restyled with digit keys, faction names
  and band words on the sheet, "The ways it ends" overlay, the people strip, the ending plate.
- **Art** (§9): 47 plates, owner-gated. **Balance** (§10): the doors harness and the five-policy
  table, bands first. **Tests** (§12): per-ending doors, unearned rows, card-only critical paths,
  v0.24.0 saves, byte-identity of the other six stories.
- **One minor release** (§17), with a checkpoint after the doors land.

## §3 — The story

### 3.1 Premise

Unchanged in substance, sharpened in shape. You wake beneath birch trees at the forest's edge
beside Edgewood, the last comfortable village before the Marches. Something patient and brass is
working outward from the Wound: not a demon but a pattern that converts living order into ticking
mechanism, crossing one ring every nine days (the Nine Count, `data/lore/the_nine_count.md`). You
are never required to stop it. You may take the bakery's work and never learn its name; you may
listen, and keep a list; you may stand where the militia counts people; you may look at the shape
long enough to name it, and then pay for the naming. The quiet life is a complete game.

The register stays the storyteller prompt's: pastoral, grounded, wry in the small things, and
honest that the evil ticks either way.

### 3.2 Three acts, following the phases

No act is a gate of its own: the arcs and phases already gate, and an act is how the spec, the
prompt and the harness talk about where a run is.

| Act | Phase (progress) | What the player meets | Doors open in it |
|---|---|---|---|

| **II — The Count** | `stirring` (0.2-0.5) | Whisper and March: Nessa's drawings, the wrong map, ward charms; Sera's levy and its clock (§5.1), the press in the square, Sera's bargain; the barrows; the first doom beats | The Long Witness; The Counted March |
| **III — The Join** | `spreading` and `consuming` (0.5-1.0) | Convergence: naming the wound, the village meeting, the ninth pin, the inward road and the join, the sacrifice; the tunnels open (0.70), the tower assembles (0.88), the margins empty | The Named Wound; The Price Asked; The Pin and the Join; The Stopped Wheel; the terminal death |

## §4 — The endings and their doors

### 4.1 The rule

- **Each ending is locked by name** (`{type: ending_lock, ending: <id>}`) in exactly the place
  listed below, and its module plays on the same beat (`{type: ending_module}`). No id-less lock
  remains in the story (asserted, §12.2). The finale quests that carried one —
  `banking_the_fire` and `the_last_quiet_thing` — lose it; `the_sacrifice`'s quest-level lock
  moves onto its card (the card can lock because the gate below reads the flags the same beat sets
  first, before the lock effect runs).
- **Each door is a deck card** in a repeatable deck whose `when:` puts it where its person stands at
  that hour, and each door card is gated on its own ending (`{ending: {eligible: <id>}}`, the
  HUE & CRY pattern, AUTHORING §3.5), so a door and its gate cannot drift apart. "Not yet" is never
  the last chance: the deck re-arms when the player walks out (`director.rearm`).
- **Two endings that can be open in one place at one time are beats of one card** (AUTHORING §3.5),
  never two cards in one hand.
- **The fail-forward has two doors**: the terminal death (§6.3), and "stay anyway" at the morning
  firing for a player whose Hearth gate is shut.

### 6.1 E1 — Probabilistic declared world events, on the day roll

`engine/world/schedules.py::declared_events_due` gains a probabilistic form. A declared event with
`probability_per_day` is rolled **once per in-game day crossed** in `(from_day, to_day]` on which
it is eligible, inside `advance_time`'s day roll, on its own stream:

```yaml
events:
  caravan_arrival:
    probability_per_day: 0.25      # one draw per eligible day crossed
    min_day: 5                     # eligible from this day
    when: { min_awareness: 0 }     # optional; asked on each day, NOT once (no fired flag)
    stream: schedule.caravan       # optional; default "schedule.<event_id>"
    duration_days: 2
    location_id: edgewood_square
    npc_ids: [npc_odran]
    rumor_from: npc_odran          # optional: a tiered rumour attributed to this NPC
    text: "Odran's cart comes in off the Millhaven road with the dust still on it."
```

- **Eligible** = not active (`_event_active`), `day >= min_day`, and `when` (if any) holds. One
  `world_rng(state, stream)` draw per eligible day; an ineligible day draws nothing. So the draws
  depend only on the seed and the days crossed, never on how the hours were cut into ticks.
- A probabilistic event **repeats**: once expired it is eligible again. A `when:` on it is a gate,
  not a once-only trigger, so it sets no `event_fired_` flag. (A `when:` event with no probability
  keeps today's fire-once meaning, byte for byte.)
- `rumor_from` draws `pick_rumor(state, rng, source_npc=...)` on the event's own stream, after the
  probability draw; the rumour lands on `state.rumors` as today.
- `stream:` exists so the flagship keeps its three existing stream names (`schedule.caravan`,
  `schedule.tinker`, `schedule.militia`, `engine/game/rng.py`): a v0.24.0 save's counters continue.
- `goods:` (read by nothing today; `engine/world/schedules.py` writes it into a payload no code
  reads) is not carried over. Dropped, not wired: the caravan's stock is Odran's vendor table.

The flagship declares `caravan_arrival` (0.25/day from day 5, as now), `tinker_camp`
(`probability_per_day: 0.0073`, the declared 5% a week as a daily rate) and `militia_press`
(0.03/day, `when: {min_awareness: 20}`), each with `text:`, and removes its three legacy top-level
blocks. It also declares the stopped wheel's door:

```yaml

    when: { value: { name: evil_progress, min: 1.0 } }   # fire-once, deterministic
    duration_days: 60
    forces_scene: the_stopped_wheel
    text: "Nothing in Edgewood keeps time any more except the thing that took it."
```

## §7 — The overlay-to-intent fix (rule 1)

Closes the GOVERNANCE row "An overlay's action as an intent" for the flagship's four overlays.

### 7.1 The protocol

- The client sends `overlay_action {session_id, intent: {action, target}, label}` over the socket
  (or `POST` the turn route with `overlay_intent` in the body; one resolver serves both).
- `default_state.resolve_overlay_intent(session, raw)` returns `(intent, sentence, refusal)`:
  refuses an `action` not in `OVERLAY_ACTIONS`; refuses a pair not in `overlay_targets(state)`
  (uncapped, read under no lock: a cheap pre-check); otherwise `sentence = describe_intent(intent)`
  (the catalogue label, never a raw id) and the turn runs through `run_guarded(session, sentence,
  intent)` exactly like a chosen chip — the hosted queue, the turn lock, the autosave.
- **A refusal before the turn** answers `turn_error {message, busy: false, stale_choice: true}`:
  nothing ran, the save is unchanged, the overlay stays open and refetches its legal list. The
  words are the engine's ("That is not on offer here and now.") or the skill's own refusal when one
  exists (`trade.refusal_to_buy`, `_craft_refusal`).
- **State that changes between the pre-check and the turn lock** (the clock moved, a coin spent
  elsewhere) is caught by the turn itself: `execute_intent` refuses and the refusal reaches the
  prose, as for any chip (rule 1, unchanged code).
- Typed text still carries no intent. `resolve_player_intent` is unchanged.

### 7.2 The verbs

`OVERLAY_ACTIONS = {buy, sell, eat, use, equip, unequip, craft, work}`.

| Verb | Skill | Legal targets (uncapped) | In the narrator's grammar? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `buy` | `trade` | `_buy`'s rows: a vendor here, in stock, affordable | yes, as today (capped at 8) |
| `sell` | `trade_sell` | `_sell`'s rows | yes, as today |
| `eat` | `eat` | `_eat`'s rows | yes, as today |
| `craft` | `craft_item` | `mechanics.craftable_here` | yes, as today |
| `work` | `work` | `economy.available` here, now | yes, as today |
| `use` | `use_item` | carried items with a `use:` block whose `inventory.use` would not refuse (not carried, no companion item, once a day) | **no** — overlay only |
| `equip` | `equip_item` | carried items with an `equip:` block not already worn | **no** — overlay only |
| `unequip` | `unequip_item` | worn items | **no** — overlay only |

The builders are refactored to produce their full list with the cap applied only where the grammar

## §11 — Saves, migration and byte-identity

### 11.1 v0.24.0 saves load and play

No state moves and `CURRENT_SAVE_VERSION` stays 2: every change is content, a new optional key, or
a new route. The pieces a save carries are kept stable on purpose:

- quest, stage and narrative-flag ids are unchanged (§4.3 keeps every stage);
- the secret places are revealed by `known_when`, never by a stored flag, so a save past
  `tunnels_open` knows `the_works` at load;
- the three events keep their ids, so a save with `caravan_arrival` active is skipped until it
  expires, and `_meta`'s first-seen days (the Whisper gate) are read as before; the streams keep
  their names, so their counters continue;
- an ending already locked stays locked (a lock is never walked back), and a save already past
  `evil_progress` 1.0 fires `the_last_hour` at its next day crossing;
- a save that took `take_it_or_dont` by leaving the pin under v0.24.0 keeps the pin it was wrongly
  given (no state is rewritten after the fact); the fix applies from the next run.

No migration step is written. If T0's fixtures (§12.4) show one is needed, it is a
`register_story_migration("clockwork-dark", 2, ...)` step and this section is amended.

### 11.2 The goldens

- `tests/test_llm_golden_lmstudio.py` replays unchanged: its storyteller messages are fixed text and
  the flagship's grammar at its seeds does not move (no new verb enters `legal_intents`; no deck or
  thread is due on day 1 in the forest clearing). **A moved recording is a design failure to fix by
  gating, never a reason to re-record** (that file's own rule).
- `tests/test_local_mode_golden.py` records the flagship's routes and payloads: each recording the
  overhaul moves (the people rows, a new route in the route list, the schedules) carries its own
  named `json_sanction`, as v0.21.0's did.

### 11.3 Byte-identity for the other six stories

For `hue-and-cry`, `neon-city`, `wicked-garden`, `the-long-con`, `dev-story` and `read-receipts`:

- `legal_intents(state)` and the rendered turn schema, at their opening and over a seeded stub run,
  equal recordings made from v0.24.0 (T0);
- a 20-turn stub-narrator run's prompt digests and save digests equal the v0.24.0 recordings
  (READ RECEIPTS: its existing turn-cost and digest pins, unchanged);
- The Wicked Garden's and Dev Story's legacy event rolls, forced and unforced, equal v0.24.0's.

## §17 — Release shape

**One minor, v0.25.0. Not split.** The engine work is five small seams; the content is cards over
quests, gates and places that already exist; the UI is four debts the GOVERNANCE table already
specifies. That is smaller than v0.23.0 (33 tasks) and shaped like it: build, checkpoint, measure,
review. Splitting into two minors would push The Wicked Garden to v0.27.0 for a release with no
player-facing half of its own. The two owner-gated tasks (T15, T16) are the only parts that can slip;
if the owner has not given the go-ahead by T17, they are recorded in CLAUDE.md's deferred list and
carried to the next release's end on the owner's word, as v0.23.0's tail was, and v1.0.0's row
(every story with its art and live play) still holds them.
